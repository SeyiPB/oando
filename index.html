The model

Three tracks, one operating standard.

Applicants choose the track that best matches their current role and intent: learn, operate, or invest.
Learn

Build fluency in ETA.

For members entering the category and needing a practical grasp of how good deals are found, evaluated, financed, and operated.

  • Deal sourcing and broker outreach habits
  • Valuation, add-backs, and risk framing
  • Diligence checklists and case study review
Operate

Work on live opportunities.

For active searchers and owners who need sharper feedback loops, better pattern recognition, and operator-caliber accountability.

  • Structured deal review rooms and critique
  • Operator AMAs and working sessions
  • Post-close support and peer accountability
Invest

Back better operators.

For capital partners who want stronger context, clearer operator quality signals, and a more intelligent view of ETA opportunities.

  • Investor education around ETA mechanics
  • Operator visibility and matching
  • Disciplined discussion around showcased deals
Why this matters

Most people trying to buy a small business are learning in public, alone, or too late.

Owners & Operators creates a better environment: a place to ask sharper questions, test judgment before capital is committed, and learn from people a few steps ahead.
A good ETA community should improve how members think, not just what they know.
The highest value comes from repeated exposure to real deals, real operators, and real decision tradeoffs.
The room works only if standards are high: discretion, seriousness, and a bias toward action.
Who it’s for

A community for people who want ownership, not just content.

Applicants may come from different stages, but the goal is the same: sharper judgment, better relationships, and a clearer path into small-business ownership.

New ETA learners

Professionals exploring entrepreneurship through acquisition who want a credible path into the category.

Active searchers and owners

Members evaluating deals, talking to brokers, raising capital, or operating after close who need better feedback.

Capital partners

Investors, lenders, and backers who want a better read on operator quality and a stronger understanding of ETA.

Community in practice

Professional, collaborative, and grounded in real work.

The interface should feel more like a serious advisory institution than a creator community: clear, restrained, and built for trust.
Community layer

Shared assets for every serious member.

The room becomes more valuable when the same high-quality layer supports everyone across stages.

  • Curated deals newsletter with learning notes
  • Member directory across operators, searchers, and capital
  • Live sessions, roundtables, and operator-led discussion
