Owners & Operators Terms and Conditions

By applying for or participating in Owners & Operators, you agree to help maintain a serious, respectful, and confidential learning environment for acquisition entrepreneurs, operators, investors, and advisors.

  1. Respectful participation: engage professionally, avoid harassment or discrimination, and contribute in good faith.
  2. Confidentiality: do not share private member discussions, deal details, documents, or personal information outside the community without permission.
  3. No professional advice: community content is educational and informational only. Members are responsible for their own legal, financial, tax, investment, and operational decisions.
  4. No spam or solicitation: do not use the community for unsolicited promotions, recruiting, fundraising, or sales activity unless expressly permitted by the organizers.
  5. Member responsibility: information you provide should be accurate, and you are responsible for your conduct, posts, and interactions.
  6. Moderation: Owners & Operators may review applications, moderate discussions, remove content, or revoke access to protect the quality and safety of the room.
  7. Materials: community materials are for member use only and may not be copied, resold, or publicly distributed without permission.
  8. Updates: these terms may be updated as the community evolves; continued participation means you accept the current terms.
